A constraint the numbers impose: one hue-rotate value cannot match both assets to the accent hue. 286deg puts the hourglass at exactly 326° but leaves computer.webp (the section and empty-state icon) at 300°; 300deg does the reverse. If exact matching matters, the tint would have to stop being one shared rule — which is a larger change than choosing a number. A1 and A3 avoid the problem by not aiming at the accent at all.

This is a mockup for a decision, not the change. Nothing in ui/src was modified: the shipping value is still hue-rotate(350deg).
